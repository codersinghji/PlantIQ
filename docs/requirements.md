# PlantIQ - AI Powered Industrial Operations Management Platform

## Project Overview

PlantIQ is a web-based industrial operations management platform designed for chemical plants, oil & gas industries, and manufacturing facilities.

The system helps engineers monitor equipment health, manage maintenance activities, and predict machine failures using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

## Problem Statement

Industrial plants contain hundreds of machines such as pumps, compressors, heat exchangers, and motors. Unexpected equipment failures can cause production downtime, financial losses, and safety risks.

PlantIQ helps organizations reduce these risks by monitoring machine health and providing predictive maintenance recommendations.

## Objectives

- Monitor industrial equipment
- Manage plant assets
- Predict machine failures using AI
- Schedule maintenance tasks
- Generate reports and analytics
- Improve equipment reliability
